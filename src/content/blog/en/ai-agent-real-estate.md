A real-estate agency that lives off demand peaks cannot afford to lose leads because of slow responses. The challenge was to handle incoming requests 24/7 without hiring more people or overloading the current team.

## Before

Every incoming request went through the same bottleneck: someone had to read it, qualify it and decide whether it was worth scheduling a visit. During rental-demand peaks the backlog piled up and many leads cooled off before the first reply.

## After

We deployed an AI agent that takes the request, asks the qualifying questions (area, budget, dates, requirements) and only escalates to the human team the leads that match the criteria. It returns an immediate reply to the client and leaves the full trail in the CRM.

## Outcome

More than three hours saved per day just on handling incoming requests, and a human team that now only schedules visits instead of triaging. Especially useful during rental-demand peaks.
