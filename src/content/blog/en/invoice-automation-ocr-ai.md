Processing invoices by hand is slow, repetitive and error-prone. By combining OCR with language models, we extract the key data from scanned invoices and push it straight into the company's systems.

## Before

Someone opened each PDF, copied amounts, dates and vendors into a spreadsheet, then entered them into the ERP. Hours of work every week, with the risk of getting a digit wrong.

## After

OCR reads the document, the model interprets and structures the fields, and a validation layer checks that the totals add up before the invoice is recorded. The team only reviews the doubtful cases.

## Result

Fewer hours spent on mechanical tasks, fewer errors and a process that scales without adding headcount. Automation does not replace the team, it frees them up for the work that adds value.
